# Search strategy

Reproduced from Appendix A of the paper. Record-level screening decisions are in [`supplement/S1_screening_log.csv`](../supplement/S1_screening_log.csv).

The search was run in OpenAlex on 21 October 2026 through its application programming interface, using the title-and-abstract search filter with no date, language or document-type limits. The four queries were (1) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND "machine learning", which returned 412 records; (2) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND IVF AND "machine learning", 129 records; (3) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND IVF AND "neural network", 50 records; and (4) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND IVF AND "random forest", 39 records. Each query was retrieved in full in pages of 200 records. Query 2 is a subset of query 1. Records were deduplicated by DOI, or by title when no DOI was available, which left 445 unique records. To test whether the machine-learning terms missed studies described in other words, five supplementary queries were run on 22 October 2026 with the same filter and limits: (5) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND IVF AND "deep learning", 60 records; (6) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND IVF AND "artificial intelligence", 163 records; (7) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND XGBoost, 88 records; (8) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND IVF AND "gradient boosting", 25 records; and (9) title_and_abstract.search:"live birth" AND IVF AND "support vector", 19 records. After removing records already found by the primary search and duplicates between queries, 124 new records remained; queries 8 and 9 added none. Scopus was searched on 22 October 2026 through Wuhan University's subscription with the advanced query TITLE-ABS-KEY ( "live birth\*" AND ( ivf OR "in vitro fertili\*" OR icsi OR "intracytoplasmic sperm injection" OR "assisted reproduct\*" ) AND ( "machine learning" OR "deep learning" OR "artificial intelligence" OR "neural network\*" OR "random forest\*" OR xgboost OR "gradient boost\*" OR "support vector" OR "decision tree\*" OR lightgbm OR catboost OR "ensemble learning" ) ), which returned 324 records. The Web of Science Core Collection was searched on the same day with the equivalent topic query TS=("live birth\*" AND (IVF OR "in vitro fertili\*" OR ICSI OR "intracytoplasmic sperm injection" OR "assisted reproduct\*") AND ("machine learning" OR "deep learning" OR "artificial intelligence" OR "neural network\*" OR "random forest\*" OR XGBoost OR "gradient boost\*" OR "support vector" OR "decision tree\*" OR LightGBM OR CatBoost OR "ensemble learning")), which returned 293 records. Both result sets were exported in full, with DOIs, and deduplicated against the OpenAlex records by DOI, or by title where no DOI existed: 146 Scopus records were already among the OpenAlex records, leaving 178 new; of the Web of Science records, 138 were among the OpenAlex records and 83 among the new Scopus records, leaving 72 new. In total, 1,602 records were identified and 783 duplicates removed. Full texts were sought from publisher websites and open-access repositories and, where not openly available, through the author's institutional library. The record identifiers, source databases, screening decisions and retrieval status of all 819 screened records, and of the record found by citation searching, are given in Supplementary File S1.
